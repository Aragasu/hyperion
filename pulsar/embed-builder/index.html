Pictures, limits and what it asks of you

Needs Manage messages in the channel you post it in.

Pictures must be https links from a host Yapper serves: imgur, Tenor, Giphy, YouTube, Twitch, Spotify, Reddit, X, GitHub, Wikimedia, AniList, MyAnimeList, nekos.best, cdn.weeb.sh, a Yapper upload, or the Yapper website.

Five cards and 6,000 characters across a whole message; this page builds one card. To post several, paste the JSON as an array — see p!embed.
